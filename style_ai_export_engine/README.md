# STYLE AI Export Sales Engine

AI B2B export sales intelligence platform for STYLE.

## Core Goals
- Upload Excel / CSV / Word / PDF customer files
- Clean and analyze company data
- Score buyers by purchase probability
- Rank Golden 500 opportunities
- Generate personalized English sales emails
- Create country-specific campaigns

## Modules
- Buyer Intelligence
- Company Scoring
- Email Intelligence
- Campaign Generator
- Analytics
